Team Helixonauts. NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Crew health monitoring that runs on a phone camera.

A short check for heart rate, breathing, alertness and thinking speed, an agent that compares each crew member with their own baseline, and the space environment from NASA data.

Try the health check

Open the QR page on this screen and scan it with your phone. The check runs in the phone's browser; the video never leaves the phone.

What is real and what is not

Measured
Camera readings and test results from the health check. Experimental; a reading is left blank when the signal is too weak.
Historical
ISS radiation and space weather from NASA, May 2024, and NASA Inspiration4 reference labs. Not live, and not about the person being scanned.
Simulated
The agent's demo crews and scenarios. Labelled as demo or simulated wherever they appear.
HALO is a prototype. It is not a medical device and its results are not medical advice.
