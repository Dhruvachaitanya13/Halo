Three parts, one system

Each one works on its own and they share the same crew data.

Health check

A one to five minute check on any phone: camera scan, docking game and three questions. It ends with a readiness score and a clear verdict: ready, caution or rest.

Start a check Talk to HALO

Ask about the crew, your last check, or radiation and space weather. By chat, by voice, or on a phone call. HALO answers only from crew data.

Open the assistant Crew Control

Who is ready today, the daily report, and a crew SOS that walks you through an emergency checklist one step at a time, hands-free if needed.

How it works

From scan to action in a few minutes.

Scan the code

A crew member scans a QR code and the check opens in the phone's browser. Nothing to install. The video never leaves the phone.

Take the check

Hold still for the camera, fly a short docking mission, answer three questions. HALO coaches you if the light or the signal is poor.

HALO compares and acts

Each result is compared with that person's own earlier checks. HALO flags what changed, suggests a check on a crewmate, and starts the SOS when asked.
