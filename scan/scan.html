Before we start

This check has a camera scan, short thinking tests and two questions about sleep.

  • The camera estimates heart rate, breathing rate and blinking. The video stays on this device and is not recorded.
  • Only the resulting numbers are saved, under a random session code, with no name.
  • The results are estimates for a demonstration. They are not medical advice.
